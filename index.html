on the mic

Radiance Rift Rumble x CCC

CCC is the Cards & Collectibles Carnival, and I manage it: the card shop and collectibles zone we ran with Anubis inside BetterTrade 2026 by efin, at Samyan Mitrtown Hall, 31 Oct and 1 Nov. Card shops, Warhammer, comics, and a Learn to Play booth for Riftbound, Magic: The Gathering and Disney Lorcana.

Radiance Rift Rumble was the Riftbound tournament that ran with it on the Saturday. I was the tournament organizer, spoke on the BetterTrade main stage, and cast the Top 8 with Jade. I also led content for the whole campaign: event graphics, the event site and the videos.
