04About

Hi, I'm Philly.

Architect by training, artist by default, researcher by habit. Bangkok-based, Japan-obsessed. I like systems, stories, and places where people gather for reasons bigger than the reason on the sign.

  • shop

    Anubis Cards & Craft

    Co-founder of a card shop, event venue and 140+ member community in Bangkok. Magic, Lorcana, Pokémon, One Piece, Riftbound. When we opened, Bangkok had no Lorcana scene. We built it table by table.

  • ai

    Breaking models, politely.

    I red team AI systems as a Japanese language specialist. Finding where models fail, so they fail less. Before that, two years of blockchain research.

  • research

    Doctor of Engineering.

    Surveyed all 70 pro baseball and football home stadiums in Japan and built a typology of how they become community space beyond game day. The University of Tokyo, MEXT scholar, 2024. Before that, an M.Arch at Chulalongkorn measuring 261 buildings in color.
